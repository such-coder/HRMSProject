The official internal platform for raising, tracking, and fulfilling hardware procurement requests across IndianOil.

How it works
1
Employee raises a request
Select hardware, provide justification and required date. Saved as draft until submitted.
2
IS GM reviews and approves
IS General Manager reviews the justification and approves or rejects with remarks.
3
Processing team fulfils the order
Procures the asset, records asset tag and serial number, marks as processed.
4
Request closed & asset delivered
Employee receives hardware and request is closed. Every step logged in audit trail.
